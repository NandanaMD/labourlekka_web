# Introduction

Labour Lekka ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Labour Lekka mobile application.

By installing or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this Policy, please discontinue use of the App.

---

# Nature of the Application

Labour Lekka is a local-first, primarily offline mobile application designed to help farmers, agricultural contractors, estates, and daily wage employers manage labour attendance (Hajari), wages, advances, and related ledger records.

- The App does not require an internet connection for its core functionality.
- All labour records are stored locally on your device by default.
- The App does not operate or maintain backend servers for storing user data.
- Users may optionally back up and restore their data using their own Google Drive account.
- Backup files are stored directly in the user's personal Google Drive account and are never stored on our servers.

---

# Information We Do Not Collect

We value your privacy. Labour Lekka does **not** collect, store, process, or transmit your labour records or personal information to our servers.

Specifically, we do **not**:

- Collect personal or business data for storage on our servers.
- Access the contents of your labour records.
- Track your activity using analytics services.
- Display advertisements.
- Share information with advertisers or marketing partners.
- Sell, rent, or monetize your data.

When you choose to use the optional Google Drive backup feature, your backup is stored directly in **your own Google Drive account**. We do not have access to, retain, or store copies of those backup files.

# Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores only the information that you voluntarily enter. This may include:

- Worker names
- Phone numbers
- Addresses
- Attendance records
- Wage details
- Advance payment records
- Farmer / Contractor / Employer information
- Work categories
- Generated reports
- Backup files
- Exported documents

All information remains under your control unless you choose to export or back up your data.

---

# How Your Information Is Used

The information stored within the App is used solely to provide its intended functionality, including:

- Managing labour attendance
- Recording wages and advances
- Calculating dues and balances
- Generating summaries and reports
- Creating local exports (PDF, Excel, etc.)
- Creating and restoring backups
- Synchronizing backups with your own Google Drive account (only when you explicitly choose to use this feature)

Your information is **never** used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or marketing purposes.

# Data Storage and Security

Labour Lekka is designed with privacy in mind.

- All data is stored locally on your device.
- The App does not upload your data to any servers operated by us.
- Optional backups can be stored in your own Google Drive account.
- Backup files remain under your ownership and control.
- We cannot access your Google Drive files.
- Optional security features such as App Lock (PIN or biometric authentication) may be available to help protect your locally stored information.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data within the App, the security of your information also depends on the security of:

- Your device
- Your Google account (if using Google Drive backup)
- Your device passcode or biometric security

# Google Drive Backup

Labour Lekka offers an **optional** Google Drive backup feature for your convenience.

If you choose to use this feature:

- You sign in using your own Google account.
- Backup files are saved directly to your personal Google Drive.
- We do not upload backups to our own servers.
- We do not read, modify, or access your backup contents.
- We do not retain copies of your backups.
- You may delete your backups from Google Drive at any time.

Use of Google Drive is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

# Data Deletion

You remain in complete control of your information.

You may delete your data by:

### Option 1

Inside the App:

1. Open **Settings**
2. Select **App Management**
3. Tap **Reset App**

### Option 2

Uninstall the application.

Uninstalling Labour Lekka removes all locally stored application data from your device (subject to your device's operating system behavior).

If you created Google Drive backups, those backups remain in your Google Drive until you choose to delete them.
